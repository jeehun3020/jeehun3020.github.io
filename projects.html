I SEE YOU — Can You Trust It by Eye Alone?

Top Excellence Award · Industry-Academia Capstone Design  /  Top Excellence Award · AI-Powered SW Startup Idea  /  Best Paper (Silver) · KIIT 2026

I SEE YOU (ISY) is a multimodal platform for checking whether content can be trusted. Most detectors answer one question about one file: is this AI-generated? ISY also asks whether the pieces of a post agree with each other — lips with voice, scene with caption — and always shows why. Detect + Verify + Explain: it returns a review-risk signal with evidence, and a person makes the final call.

Why now: in April 2026 an AI-generated sighting photo of a wolf that escaped a Daejeon zoo widened the search and closed nearby schools, and marketplace scams now forge product photos with generative AI. Korea’s AI Basic Act (Jan. 2026) requires labels on AI output, but labels vanish when content is cropped or re-uploaded. ISY looks at the content itself.

How It Works

  • Image — EfficientNet-B4 with separate RGB and FFT streams joined by late fusion; high-frequency spikes reveal generation traces. Grad-CAM heatmap as evidence.
  • Video (my module) — a seven-model EfficientNet-B0 frame ensemble with a text mask so subtitles and watermarks can’t become shortcuts, plus frame-difference temporal features. Flags suspicious segments on a timeline.
  • Text — English: DeBERTa-v3 with LoRA. Korean: the LOG-AID method — six logit statistics (surprisal, entropy, JSD, log-rank…) from Qwen2.5-7B into logistic regression. Evidence per sentence.
  • Multimodal consistency — OpenCLIP and FLAVA (frame ↔ text), BLIP + NLI (caption ↔ scene logic), AVSync (lips ↔ voice), frequency fusion and a SceneGraph GCN. A pre-check scores input quality so blurry or silent inputs carry less weight.
  • Fusion — each usable modality’s fake probability and the cross-modal inconsistency risk are combined by reliability-weighted averaging into one review-risk score.

TrendDo — From Trends You Watch to Culture You Live

KHUTHON 2026 Excellence Award

TrendDo is an AI culture-circulation platform that translates trends into the language of each generation — trends that are consumed and forgotten at short-form speed — and converts them into actionable ToDo challenges and local / traditional culture experiences.

"Algorithms make people watch longer, but they don't make culture experienced more deeply." — TrendDo closes that gap with a four-stage loop.

The Four-Stage Culture Loop

  • 1. Discover (Trend Radar) — automatic trend collection from YouTube, Naver, and seed data
  • 2. Understand (Generation Guide) — translated for teens / 30s–40s / 50s–60s / families / foreign visitors
  • 3. Act (Trend-Do Card) — converted into ToDo challenges with materials, time, cost, and steps
  • 4. Reproduce (K-Culture Map / Admin Ops) — fused with local assets and traditional culture, turned into cultural data

Core AI Features

  • OpenAI Responses API + Structured Output — LLM output validated with a Zod schema; fallbacks logged as provenance_label=demo_seed
  • Explainable regional matching score — six factors: category, action, material, place, schedule, and safety
  • Safety Gate — screens for portrait rights, personal data, risky behavior, and cultural distortion; high-risk items are blocked from auto-approval
  • AI Ops Log — tracks input, output, model name, prompt version, and generation time for every LLM run
  • Municipal proposal email drafts — generated in a review state, never auto-sent before approval

Regional Fusion Examples

  • Dubai chewy cookie + Gangwon → chewy potato-tteok dessert challenge
  • Photo challenge + Jeonju → hanok alley one-shot remix
  • Running crew + Jinju → Namgang lantern night move course
  • Decoration trend + traditional craft → mother-of-pearl pattern keyring remix
