Recognition
우수논문상 (은상), First Author, 심화캡스톤 경진대회 최우수상

Abstract

Existing AI-generated content detection methods have not sufficiently judged semantic discrepancies between modals because they analyze images, videos, and texts individually. Subsequent studies have developed frequency-based image detection, frame and time axis-based image detection, and language model-based text detection methods, and the detection performance has improved in each modal. In this paper, we propose a system that combines single modal detection results and multimodal consistency analysis to improve the accuracy and interpretability of AI-generated content verification.
