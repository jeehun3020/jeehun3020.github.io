Authors
Jihoon Jeong, Sieun Kim, Yeeun Kim, Eunseo Park, Seunga Seo, Seoryeong Jang, Kiyeon Ham

Conference
한국정보기술학회 하계 종합학술대회 논문집

Year
2025

Abstract

This paper presents a vision-based learning concentration analysis system for intelligent study environments. The proposed system analyzes facial expressions and behavioral cues such as eye closure, yawning, head movement, and seat departure to quantitatively evaluate learner focus levels.

Abstract
